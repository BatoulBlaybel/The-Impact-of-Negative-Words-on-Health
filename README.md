# 🧠 The Impact of Negative Words on Health

## 📌 Project Status
🚧 **Ongoing**  

## 📖 Overview

This research study investigates the impact of negative words on health outcomes by exploring how language patterns and emotional expressions may influence individual well-being.

The study focuses on collecting and analyzing survey responses to better understand the potential relationship between negative language exposure, psychological factors, and health-related outcomes.

Data collection is currently ongoing, and participation through the survey will contribute valuable insights to support this research.

🔗 [Participate in the Survey](https://ee.kobotoolbox.org/x/cjn5yO8j)

## 🛠️ Methodology

- Survey design and data collection
- Statistical analysis of collected responses
- Exploration of relationships between language patterns and health outcomes
- Data-driven interpretation of findings
